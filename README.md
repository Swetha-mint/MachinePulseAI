# MachinePulse AI

### Intelligent Conveyor Belt Health Monitoring & Rupture Prediction

> **Predict Before the Belt Fails.**

MachinePulse AI is an intelligent conveyor belt condition-monitoring concept designed to continuously observe machine behaviour, identify abnormal patterns, and support early maintenance decisions using multimodal sensing and AI/ML.

---

## 🚨 The Problem

Conveyor belts are critical to industrial operations, especially in environments such as mining and manufacturing.

Unexpected belt damage or rupture can lead to:

- Unplanned downtime
- Production disruption
- Maintenance delays
- Increased operational costs

Traditional maintenance can often become reactive:

**Failure → Shutdown → Inspection → Repair**

MachinePulse AI aims to move towards:

**Monitor → Detect → Assess → Alert → Maintain**

---

## 💡 Our Solution

MachinePulse AI combines physical machine monitoring, sensor data, edge processing and AI/ML analysis into one integrated system.

The system is designed to:

1. Continuously monitor conveyor operating conditions
2. Collect data from multiple sensing parameters
3. Process and analyse machine behaviour
4. Identify abnormal operating patterns
5. Assess conveyor health and potential risk
6. Provide understandable alerts and monitoring insights
7. Support predictive-maintenance decisions

📡 Multimodal Monitoring

Different sensors provide different views of machine behaviour.

Parameter	What it can indicate
Vibration	Mechanical behaviour
Acoustic signal	Operating-condition changes
Temperature	Thermal behaviour
Motor current / load	Operating effort
Belt speed	Operating context
Vision*	Visible belt abnormalities

* Vision is a planned/possible sensing modality and will only be marked as implemented once integrated into the prototype.

The final sensor configuration will be documented as the prototype develops.

🧠 AI / ML

The AI/ML layer is intended to analyse machine-condition data and identify deviations from expected operating behaviour.

A key approach being explored is anomaly detection:

Normal Operating Behaviour
          ↓
Learn / Establish Pattern
          ↓
New Sensor Data
          ↓
Compare With Expected Behaviour
          ↓
      Deviation?
       /      \
     NO        YES
     ↓          ↓
  NORMAL     ANOMALY
                 ↓
          Risk Assessment

An anomaly does not automatically mean a rupture. It indicates that the observed behaviour differs from the expected operating pattern and may require further assessment.
